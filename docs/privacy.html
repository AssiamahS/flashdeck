Flash Deck — Privacy Policy

Effective: July 16, 2026

What the skill stores

Flash Deck stores two things, keyed to your anonymous Alexa user ID: your study progress (which cards you got right or wrong, used for spaced repetition) and the voice notes you explicitly save with the "note" command. This data lives in the skill's private cloud storage and is used only to run the skill.

What the skill does not do

  • No collection of names, emails, contacts, or location.
  • No advertising, no analytics trackers, no sale or sharing of data.
  • No audio is stored by the skill — voice processing is handled by Amazon Alexa under Amazon's privacy policy.

Deck content

Flashcard decks are loaded from a public GitHub repository. Anything you add through the deck editor is committed to that repository under your own GitHub account, using a token that stays on your device.

Deleting your data

Disabling the skill stops all storage. To have stored progress and notes erased, open an issue at github.com/AssiamahS/flashdeck/issues.
