Cards that move

A flashcard can loop an animation — watch the squat while you name the muscles, see the flag while you recall the capital. Any GIF becomes a card with one command, and it's on your Show a minute later.

🧠

Spaced repetition

Grade yourself with "got it" or "missed it". Leitner boxes bring missed cards back sooner — your weak spots get the reps.

📱

Edit from your phone

Add cards and whole decks from the web editor. Changes are live on the Show in about a minute — no redeploys, no app store.

🎞️

Animated cards

Images and looping video on cards. Exercise form, sign language, chemistry reactions — anything that moves.

📌

Voice post-its

"Note — buy protein powder." Saved to your My Notes deck instantly. Your kitchen's sticky-note board, minus the paper.

🗣️

Voice or touch

Everything works both ways: tap a deck tile or say its name, tap a card to flip it or just say "flip".

📊

Progress on the home screen

Decks, card counts, and how many you've mastered — right on the menu every time you open it.
